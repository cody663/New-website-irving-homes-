Our Story

Local Niagara Craftsmanship

Irving Homes Inc is a family-run business based in the Niagara Region of Ontario, and we only renovate homes here in Niagara. We take immense pride in our customer service, the quality of our work, and our ability to exceed our neighbours' expectations.

Our company is composed of a team of highly skilled millwork designers, carpenters, flooring experts, painters, and drywall professionals who take pride in providing comprehensive renovation and construction services to our clients.

We specialize in transforming homes into beautiful, functional spaces that enhance your lifestyle. Whether you're looking to renovate your kitchen, finish your basement, or take on a comprehensive residential renovation, we've got you covered with craftsmanship designed to stand the test of time.
