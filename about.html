Our Story

Local Niagara Craftsmanship

Irving Homes Inc is a family-run business based in the Niagara Region of Ontario, and we only renovate homes here in Niagara. We take immense pride in our customer service, the quality of our work, and our ability to exceed our neighbours' expectations.

Our company is composed of a team of highly skilled millwork designers, carpenters, flooring experts, painters, and drywall professionals who take pride in providing comprehensive renovation and construction services to our clients.

We specialize in transforming homes into beautiful, functional spaces that enhance your lifestyle. Whether you're looking to renovate your kitchen, finish your basement, or take on a comprehensive residential renovation, we've got you covered with craftsmanship designed to stand the test of time.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every decision we make, from hiring our crew to how we communicate with clients, is guided by these principles.

Clear Communication

We believe great renovations start with great communication. From the first consultation to the final walkthrough, we keep our clients informed, involved, and confident every step of the way. No confusion, no surprises—just honest conversations and clear expectations.

Transparency & Trust

Trust is earned through transparency. We're upfront about timelines, pricing, and project details so homeowners always know exactly what to expect. Our goal is to build lasting relationships through honesty and accountability.

Uncompromising Quality

We don't cut corners. Every project is built with precision, craftsmanship, and materials that are made to last. Our team takes pride in delivering renovations that not only look beautiful but perform for years to come.

The Best Trades & Craftsmanship

We work with skilled professionals who truly care about their work. Our renovators and tradespeople are chosen not only for their expertise but for their commitment to doing the job right and taking pride in the finished result.

Supporting Local Trades & Community

We believe in strengthening the community we serve. By partnering with trusted local trades and suppliers, we support local businesses while ensuring the highest standards of workmanship.

A Stress-Free Renovation Experience

Renovations shouldn't feel overwhelming. Our team handles the details, coordinates the work, and keeps projects running smoothly so homeowners can enjoy the process instead of worrying about it.

Community First

Supporting SickKids & Families
in Our Community

Giving back is deeply important to us. Irving Homes Inc. proudly supports SickKids Hospital, with over $85,000 donated to SickKids Cardiac Care to date.

This cause is very personal to our family. We’ve seen firsthand how important SickKids is for children and families facing serious health challenges.

When you choose Irving Homes for your renovation, you’re also helping us continue to support this incredible hospital and the families who rely on it.
